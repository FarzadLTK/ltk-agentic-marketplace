Design notes (why the simulation behaves this way)
  • Offers are typed objects validated by an LTK referee against each creator's floor before anything settles. Weak negotiators accept money-losing contracts ~19% of the time without this guardrail (Liang & Xu 2026).
  • One brand agent negotiates with all creator agents in parallel. More counterparties produce faster, better deals (AgenticPay 2026). The buyer side is structurally weaker, so the brand agent follows an explicit allocation strategy, not a single chat.
  • Both sides run the same negotiation policy and configured patience. Provider identity and prompted patience, not raw capability, decide who captures surplus (Liang & Xu 2026).
  • Creator agents source from the content bank first and ask the human only when fee + projected commission beats the creator's time at their hourly floor.
  • Every click mints an attribution credential so commission is credited even if a personal agent, not a human, completes the purchase (South et al. 2025; Poppy 0.3).
